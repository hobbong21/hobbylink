## 2. Information we collect

### Automatic

- Cookies and similar storage (see [Cookies](/cookies))
- Service usage: pages visited, features used, match/event interactions

  grant permission

### From third parties

- Stripe returns payment confirmation events

## 4. How we share information

We share data only as follows, never "sell" as defined under CCPA:

- **Sub-processors** (see [Sub-Processors](/sub-processors)) operate our
  infrastructure under DPAs with SCCs where applicable.
- **Legal authorities** when required by valid legal process.
- **Successors** in a merger, acquisition, or sale, subject to continuity
  of this Policy.
- **Other users** as intended by features (e.g., profiles, messages,
  meetup participant lists).

We do **not** sell personal information or share it for cross-context
behavioral advertising.

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor)

## 6. Retention

- Account data: until you delete your account, then deleted within 30 days.
- Transaction records: **5 years** (Korea e-commerce law, also meets GDPR
  "legal obligation" exemption).
- Server logs: **90 days** rolling.
- Backups are retained for **7 days** and overwritten.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have rights:

### GDPR (EEA/UK)
- Right of **access** (Art. 15)
- Right to **rectification** (Art. 16)
- Right to **erasure** (Art. 17)
- Right to **restrict processing** (Art. 18)
- Right to **data portability** (Art. 20)
- Right to **object** (Art. 21)
- Right to **withdraw consent** at any time
- Right to **lodge a complaint** with your supervisory authority

### CCPA/CPRA (California)
- Right to **know** what PI is collected
- Right to **delete**
- Right to **correct**
- Right to **opt-out of sale/sharing** (we don't sell — banner nonetheless
  provided)
- Right to **limit use of sensitive PI**
- Right to **non-discrimination** for exercising rights

### Exercise your rights
Email {{DPO_EMAIL}} with a description of your request. We respond within
**30 days** (GDPR) / **45 days** (CCPA, extendable to 90 with notice).

## 8. Security

We use TLS 1.3 in transit, bcrypt for passwords, role-based access controls,
and row-level security policies enforced at the database. We monitor for
unauthorized access and will notify you and applicable authorities within
**72 hours** of a confirmed breach affecting your personal data.
